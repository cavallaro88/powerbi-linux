The published preview remains RPM 13. Automatic downloads and release certification
remain gated; see [installation availability](../user/install.md).

  CI/license/platform badges and beta warning in the README.

  Preserve historical release notes; remove the duplicate Portuguese guide.

  retain explicit local installer/runtime inputs. Automatic downloads remain
  unavailable until the manifest identifies a validated published combination.
